# NNNN: Title in sentence case

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Rejected
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decided by**: the owner, or a maintainer of this repository
- **Amends**: a record number, or none

## Context

What is true now that makes a decision necessary. Facts, not preferences: what exists, what
does not, what breaks without a decision. Link the code, the contract, or the pull request
that shows it.

## Decision

What was decided, in the present tense, in a few sentences. A reader should be able to act on
this section alone.

## Consequences

What follows, including what is now harder, what is now impossible, and what must be watched.
Every cost named here is one we accept knowingly.

## Alternatives

What else was considered, and why it was not taken. One short paragraph each: the option, its
appeal, the reason it lost.
